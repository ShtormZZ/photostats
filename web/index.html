Timeline

Choose a filter first

Timeline filters are independent of Statistics. Match any selected tag; no selection means all tags. Within each day, 5+ photos come first, other photos follow, and bad photos come last, after unrated. Each group is ordered from earliest to latest. 15 per day limits the results after all filters; Show all reveals every remaining match. Turn the limit off to show all matches automatically. Portfolio uses the full width, showing 5+ first, then five stars, then all other photos, each group by capture time. 5+ and five-star photos preserve their proportions and show comments at the bottom of the image. Their shorter side spans three ordinary tiles plus two gaps, including in short days. Ordinary photos fill the space beside them in aligned columns. Days share one mosaic with date tiles, no day buttons or Show all. The daily limit shows exactly 15 photos, excluding the date tile; shorter days show all matches. Choose at most one extra filter and one option; search to find more options. Focal lengths use 35mm-equivalent values. Analyse day always opens the whole day in Statistics. These filters reset when the page is reloaded.

Add tags

Existing tags are kept. Up to 3 tags per photo; duplicates are ignored.

Scanned folders

Counts and sizes include indexed photos on disk in each folder and its subfolders. Colour + sharpness counts files with both measurements available. Empty folders are not recorded. Analyse opens Statistics for the folder and its subfolders, keeping your other filters. Use Refresh to see new scan results.
