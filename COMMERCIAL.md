# Commercial licensing

photostats is free software under the [GNU Affero General Public License v3](LICENSE).
That licence is deliberately strong: if you build on this code, or run it as a
service that other people reach over a network, you have to release your whole
work — including your changes — under the AGPL as well.

For most people that is no burden at all. Use it on your own photos, change it,
share it, publish your fork: nothing to pay, nothing to ask.

## When you need a different licence

Buy a commercial licence if you want to use this code **without** the AGPL's
obligation to publish your own source. Typically:

- building it into a product you ship or sell;
- running it as a hosted service for your customers;
- using it inside a company whose policy forbids AGPL code;
- keeping your modifications private.

A commercial licence gives you the same code under ordinary proprietary terms:
no copyleft, no obligation to publish anything.

## Getting one

Write to **sergey83@gmail.com** — a couple of sentences about who you are and
what you want to do with it is enough to start. Terms are per-organisation and
negotiable.

Not sure which side of the line you are on? Ask. A short question costs you
nothing and a straight answer is better for both of us than a guess.
